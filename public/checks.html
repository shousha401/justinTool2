Sanity Check

Catches production batches whose numbers can't physically be true — before anyone reports off them. The big one: a cut or grind that ends with more weight than it started (yield over 100%). Also flags batches that lost almost all their weight, made something from no input, or list the same item as both an input and an output. All read-only from Swarmbox.
